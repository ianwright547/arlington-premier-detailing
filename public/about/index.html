Our Story

A detailing shop with a real repair shop behind it.

Arlington Premier Auto Detailing is the detailing division of TAB Motors, the AAA Approved Auto Repair shop at 4035 Old Dominion Dr in Arlington. Our neighbors already trust the shop with brakes and state inspections. Now the same location handles the look and feel of the car too.

We built the service around one idea: nobody should have to guess what a detail costs. Every package is listed by vehicle size with the time it takes, and every add-on has a posted range. You pick what the car needs, drop it off, and pick it up clean.
