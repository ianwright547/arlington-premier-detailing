How Much Does Car Detailing Cost in Arlington, VA?

PricingUpdated November 2026

The short answer is that it depends on how much work the car needs. At our shop, prices start at $49 for an Express Clean on a sedan and go up to $299 for a Premium Full Detail on an SUV or truck. Here is what you get at each level and why the price moves.

What each level includes

An Express Clean ($49 sedan, $69 SUV or truck) is a hand wash, wheels, a quick vacuum, a dash wipe-down and interior windows. A Basic Detail ($129 and $159) adds a full vacuum, door panels, door jambs and light trim.

A Premium Full Detail ($249 and $299) adds shampoo and extraction, leather care, steam, bug and tar removal, a clay bar and hand wax or spray sealant. If only one half of the car needs help, an Interior Deep Detail ($149 and $189) or Exterior Detail ($119 and $149) lets you pay for just that part.

What changes the price

Size is the first factor, which is why we price by sedan and by SUV or truck. Condition is the second. Heavy pet hair, stains and odors take more time, so those are handled as add-ons with posted ranges. We confirm the price before we start.

Is a full detail worth it?

If you have not had the car professionally cleaned in a year or more, a full detail usually makes more sense than a series of smaller cleans. After that, you can keep it up with an Express Clean every few weeks.

Watch out for vague quotes

Ask any shop what is actually included. The word detail can mean anything from a wash and vacuum to a full day of work. Our packages are listed line by line so you can compare.
