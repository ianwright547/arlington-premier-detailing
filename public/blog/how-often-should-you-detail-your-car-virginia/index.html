How Often Should You Detail Your Car in Northern Virginia?

MaintenanceUpdated November 2026

There is no single right answer, but there is a sensible rhythm. For most drivers in Arlington and Fairfax County, that means a light clean every few weeks and a deeper detail twice a year.

Parked outside

Street-parked cars collect sap, pollen and bird droppings all year. Plan on an Express Clean every two to three weeks and an Exterior Detail with fresh wax or sealant every four to six months.

Parked in a garage

Garage parking protects paint from sun and trees, so you can stretch things out. An Express Clean once a month and a full detail once or twice a year is plenty for most cars.

Kids and pets

Interiors with kids or dogs in them need more attention than the outside does. An Interior Deep Detail every four to six months keeps smells and stains from setting in.

The two best times of year

Late fall and early spring matter most here. In the fall, a coat of wax or sealant puts protection down before salt season. In spring, a full detail clears out winter salt and pollen.

The bottom line

If your paint feels rough after a wash, it needs clay. If water stops beading, it needs protection. If the cabin smells stale, it needs an interior clean. Your car usually tells you when it is time.
