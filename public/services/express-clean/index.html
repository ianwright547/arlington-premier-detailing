The Service

A real hand wash on a lunch break schedule.

The Express Clean is our most popular way to keep up with a daily driver. We wash the exterior by hand, clean the wheels and tires, vacuum the interior, wipe down the dash and console, clean the inside of the windows and finish with tire dressing.

It is not a full detail, and it does not try to be. Think of it as the maintenance visit between bigger services, and a far gentler option than an automatic brush wash.

How We Work

The order matters.

We follow the same sequence on every Express Clean at our Arlington shop, because skipped prep shows in the finish.

01

Check-in and walk-around

We look over the car with you and note problem areas, stains or anything you want left alone.

02

Hand wash and wheels

The car is washed and dried by hand, and wheels and tires are cleaned.

03

Quick interior

Vacuum, dash and console wipe-down, and interior glass.

04

Dressing

Tires are dressed for a clean finish.

05

Final inspection and pickup

We check the work under good light, then walk the car with you at pickup.

FAQ

Express Clean Questions

What Arlington drivers ask us most.

How long does an Express Clean take?

About 45 to 60 minutes for most vehicles.

How much is an Express Clean?

$49 for a sedan and $69 for an SUV or truck.

Is an Express Clean the same as a car wash?

It is closer to a hand wash plus a quick interior clean. There are no brushes, and a person does the work.

How often should I book one?

Every two to four weeks works well for a daily driver in Arlington.
