The Service

Everything you touch, breathe and sit on.

Your interior is the part of the car you actually live in. The Interior Deep Detail covers the complete cabin: seats, carpets, trunk, dash, console, door panels and interior windows, with carpet and upholstery shampoo throughout.

Leather gets conditioned so it stays soft, and we treat odors instead of covering them with fragrance. If the outside of the car is in good shape and the inside is not, this is the package to book.

How We Work

The order matters.

We follow the same sequence on every Interior Deep Detail at our Arlington shop, because skipped prep shows in the finish.

01

Check-in and walk-around

We look over the car with you and note problem areas, stains or anything you want left alone.

02

Remove and vacuum

Loose items and mats come out, and the entire cabin is vacuumed.

03

Shampoo

Carpets and upholstery are shampooed and extracted.

04

Panels, leather and glass

Hard surfaces are cleaned, leather is conditioned and interior windows are done.

05

Final inspection and pickup

We check the work under good light, then walk the car with you at pickup.

FAQ

Interior Deep Detail Questions

What Arlington drivers ask us most.

Can you get rid of smells?

We remove the source first, then treat what is left. Smoke and heavy odors can take more work, so tell us when you book.

How much is an interior detail?

$149 for a sedan and $189 for an SUV or truck.

How long does the interior take to dry?

Seats and carpets are usually close to dry at pickup. Leave the windows cracked if it is a warm day.

Is pet hair included?

Normal hair is vacuumed up. Heavy, woven-in pet hair is a $30 to $60 add-on.
