The Service

Wheels that stay clean longer.

Brake dust is hot, sharp and hard on wheel finishes. A ceramic sealant adds a slick protective layer to the wheel face, so dust and grime have a harder time sticking and rinse off more easily.

This add-on covers the wheels only. It is not a full paint ceramic coating.

FAQ

Wheel Ceramic Sealant Questions

What Arlington drivers ask us most.

Is this a ceramic coating?

It is a ceramic-based sealant for wheels, not a full-vehicle ceramic coating.

How much does it cost?

$50 to $100, depending on wheel size and design.

Does it stop brake dust?

No. Dust still lands on the wheel, but it sticks less and washes off more easily.
