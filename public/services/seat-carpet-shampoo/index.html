The Service

Lift out what a vacuum cannot.

Fabric seats and carpet hold on to everything. We shampoo the fabric, agitate it, and extract the dirty water out with an extractor, instead of just wetting the surface.

Seat shampoo and carpet shampoo are priced separately at $40 to $70 each, and both are already included in the Interior Deep Detail and Premium Full Detail.

How We Work

The order matters.

We follow the same sequence on every Seat and Carpet Shampoo at our Arlington shop, because skipped prep shows in the finish.

01

Check-in and walk-around

We look over the car with you and note problem areas, stains or anything you want left alone.

02

Vacuum

Dry dirt comes out first, so shampoo can reach the fibers.

03

Shampoo and agitate

Product is worked into the fabric.

04

Extract

An extractor pulls out the dirty water.

05

Final inspection and pickup

We check the work under good light, then walk the car with you at pickup.

FAQ

Seat and Carpet Shampoo Questions

What Arlington drivers ask us most.

Will it remove all stains?

Most food, drink and salt stains come out. Dye, ink and old set-in stains may lighten without fully disappearing.

How long until the seats are dry?

Usually a few hours. Airflow helps, so crack the windows on a warm day.

Can you remove road salt stains?

Yes. White salt lines on carpets are one of the most common things we extract in winter.
