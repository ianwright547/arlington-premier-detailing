The Service

The whole car, reset in one day.

The Premium Full Detail is everything we do in one package. It starts with the Basic Detail, then adds deep interior cleaning and shampoo, carpet and upholstery extraction, leather cleaning and conditioning, and steam cleaning for vents, crevices and seats.

Outside, we take off bugs, tar and road grime, run a clay bar over the paint to remove what washing leaves behind, then protect it with hand wax or spray sealant. Tires and trim get an enhanced treatment, and odors are dealt with at the source.

This is the one to book before selling a car, after a long winter, or when you simply want it to feel like the day you bought it.

How We Work

The order matters.

We follow the same sequence on every Premium Full Detail at our Arlington shop, because skipped prep shows in the finish.

01

Check-in and walk-around

We look over the car with you and note problem areas, stains or anything you want left alone.

02

Exterior wash and decontamination

Hand wash, bug and tar removal, and a clay bar pass across the paint.

03

Interior deep clean

Vacuum, shampoo, extraction and steam across seats, carpets, vents and crevices.

04

Leather and protection

Leather is cleaned and conditioned, and paint gets hand wax or spray sealant.

05

Tires, trim and glass

Enhanced tire and trim treatment, then interior and exterior glass.

06

Final inspection and pickup

We check the work under good light, then walk the car with you at pickup.

FAQ

Premium Full Detail Questions

What Arlington drivers ask us most.

How long does a full detail take?

Between 3.5 and 5 hours, depending on size and condition.

How much is a full detail in Arlington?

Our Premium Full Detail is $249 for a sedan and $299 for an SUV or truck.

Does it remove scratches?

It is not a scratch removal service. Adding the One-Step Paint Polish reduces light swirls and restores gloss.

Is it worth doing before selling my car?

A clean car shows better to buyers and at trade-in. It is the package we suggest when you are getting a car ready to sell.
