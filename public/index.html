Built for Northern Virginia

What Arlington Does to Your Car

Every season here brings its own mess. These are the ones we see most.

Road Salt and Brine

Winter treatment on I-66, Route 50 and Glebe Road sticks to rockers, wheels and door jambs. A hand wash and clay treatment gets it off before it sits.

Spring Pollen

Northern Virginia pollen coats paint and fills cowl vents and cabin carpets. Our interior packages pull it out of the places a car wash never reaches.

Tree Sap and Birds

Arlington's tree canopy is great until you park under it. Sap and droppings etch clear coat, so clay and protection matter here.

Summer Humidity

Spilled coffee and damp floor mats turn into odors fast in July. Carpet and seat shampoo removes the source, not just the smell.

Commuter Grime

Stop-and-go traffic bakes brake dust into wheels and road film into paint. Exterior packages include full wheel and tire cleaning.

Pets and Kids

Hair, crumbs and stains are what most interiors really need help with. Pet hair removal starts at $30 and pairs with any package.

FAQ

Auto Detailing Questions

Straight answers about pricing, timing and what to expect.

How much does auto detailing cost in Arlington?

Our packages run from $49 for an Express Clean to $299 for a Premium Full Detail on an SUV or truck. Add-ons run from $30 to $250.

How long does a full detail take?

A Premium Full Detail takes about 3.5 to 5 hours. An Express Clean takes about 45 to 60 minutes.

Do I need an appointment?

Please call ahead. Longer packages take several hours, so booking makes sure we have the time.

Do you come to me?

No. All detailing is done at our shop at 4035 Old Dominion Dr in Arlington.

Why does an SUV or truck cost more?

There is more surface to wash, more interior to clean, and often a third row or truck bed.

Which package is right for me?

For upkeep, choose Express Clean. For a solid clean, choose Basic Detail. For the full reset, choose Premium Full Detail. Call us if you want a recommendation.

Are you connected to TAB Motors?

Yes. Pristine Auto Detailing Arlington is the detailing side of TAB Motors, at the same location.
